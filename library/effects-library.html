Effects library

Every effect this kit can render. Each still is one frame of a moving effect, and every one is used only when it matches what is being said. 62 effects so far, and you can add your own. The line in quotes under each name is what to call it out loud: ask for “cards spinning around your head” and you will get it. The stills below were rendered on the kit’s test reel; re-render them on your own footage and in your own brand any time with node library/build.mjs --project <your slug>.
