# Reel Studio changelog

## 1.0.4 (23 Oct 2026)
- Three starter looks: Editorial, Bold and Soft. Pick one in the style picker for a single reel, or make one your default with `node engine/reel.mjs look use editorial`. Each brings its own fonts (free to use), colours and pace.
- My style: a page that shows everything the editor has learned about how you like your reels, with a Yes or Not for me on each new rule it has noticed in your notes.

## 1.0.3 (23 Oct 2026)
- Try another look: tap any moment on your Moments board and see three other ways to do it, then swap in the ones you like.
- A new design mode (set "design": 2): things snap in and cut out cleanly, your key word sits much bigger than the line around it, and jump cuts become gentle camera punch-ins.
- New effect: Blue haze softens the room behind you in your brand colour so your words pop. Best used once or twice a reel.
- Big words now size themselves to fit the frame, and can carry a tiny label above them.
- Dims, panels and the comment box now follow your own brand colours.
- Tidier download: old working files are gone from the kit.

## 1.0.2 (22 Oct 2026)
- The video gets the full height of the screen again; the note box, notes and timeline sit together on the right.
- Hide timeline: fold the timeline away while you write notes (it remembers your choice).

## 1.0.1 (22 Oct 2026)
- The review screen is now a fixed workspace on a computer: the video, the note box and the timeline stay on screen, and your notes scroll in their own column with Notes, Change and Editor tabs. Press Enter to add a note.
- Your changes waiting for the next version now sit in a slim bar at the top.
- On a phone, the video stays at the top while you scroll and the note box sits at the bottom of the screen.

## 1.0.0 (21 Oct 2026)
- Your studio is now where everything happens: type /edit and the plan, your yes or change, the moments and the cut all live on the page.
- Cut first (optional, per reel): see the cut line by line with a plain preview, keep or cut each line, then styling starts.
- Same as last time: one tick box on the style step reuses your last reel's look, just for that reel.
- Taste test: about 20 quick rounds of three effects. Your picks go into your brand guide. Run it again any time.
- Make me a cover: three cover options from moments where you're not mid-word, checked for Instagram's grid and buttons.
- B-roll organiser: sort your camera roll into a few big albums (or folders on Windows) once, and the editor pulls matching clips from them when a line needs footage.
- What to say: a one-page card of the phrases that get the best results.
- Tap to change: tap any block on the timeline (or Change on a moment) to move it earlier or later, make it shorter or longer, remove it, or preview just that bit. Or drag it along the timeline.
- Colour: eight colour looks shown on your own footage, with a strength slider, for the whole reel or just one moment. Your text and effects keep their colours.
- Fix a caption by tapping the word: correct the spelling (saved to My words for every reel) or make it the punch word.
- Your shelf: drop your logo, screenshots and photos in once and add them to any moment of any reel.
- Pick your hook: three hook options rendered on your footage before the full cut. The other two are kept as trial-reel openings.
- Compare: two versions side by side, playing together.
- A soft chime when your plan, moments or cut is ready, so you can walk away.
- Fixed: pressing the space bar while typing a note no longer plays the reel.
- Setup now starts with "build my studio".

## 0.9.0 (21 Oct 2026)
- Glow captions: each word lights up as you say it. Captions can now change style part way through a reel.
- Headline wrap: big stacked lines, the top ones behind your head and the rest in front.
- Moments: every on-screen moment as a numbered still before the full render, with a few-second preview of any one.
- Energy: pick Quiet, Balanced or Full for each reel.
- My words: names the transcript gets wrong are fixed in every reel.
- Quick fixes on the review page, a Download button, and a private link for your phone.
- Windows 10/11 support (a few effects are Mac only for now, marked "Mac" in the style picker).
