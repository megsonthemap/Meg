# Your b-roll organiser

B-roll is the footage that plays while you keep talking: you at the laptop when you say "while I'm
working", the mic and lights when you mention recording, a coffee run when the story turns personal.
Good b-roll makes a reel feel real. The trouble is finding it. It is buried in a camera roll of
thousands of photos, kids, receipts and screenshots.

This sorts it once into a few big buckets, so it is always there when a reel needs it.

## What you get

- **A few buckets, not a filing system.** Three to five, in your words. The usual ones are Me
  working, Behind the scenes and Life, plus Product and clients if that suits your business.
- **On a Mac with Apple Photos:** each bucket becomes an album in Photos, so it syncs to your phone
  and you can grab b-roll anywhere.
- **On Windows, or if your footage lives in folders:** one folder per bucket. Your clips are linked
  into them, so they take no extra space, and the originals never move.
- **An editor that uses them.** When a line in your reel wants footage behind it, Claude looks in
  the right bucket, brings in a few real clips, tells you what each one shows and lets you pick.
  It never guesses and never drops in a clip it has not looked at.

## How to start

In Claude, in your Reel Studio folder, say:

> Organise my b-roll.

Claude asks a few quick questions (where your footage is, which buckets you want, whether to leave
out photos of your kids), then shows you how many clips would go in each bucket, with a preview, before
anything is made. Nothing is created until you say yes.

## Good to know

- **It only adds.** Nothing is ever moved, deleted or taken out of an album.
- **What counts as b-roll:** clips straight off your camera or phone from 2023 on. Screenshots,
  screen recordings, finished reels and things people sent you are left out. Want older footage, or
  photos as well as video? Just say so.
- **The first time on a Mac** Claude installs a small helper inside your Reel Studio folder to read
  Photos (a minute or two). If macOS says it cannot read your library, Claude walks you to the one
  switch to turn on: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access › Claude. And when it
  makes the albums, macOS asks whether Claude can control Photos. Click OK.
- **Your face helps.** If you have named yourself in Photos (People › your face › Add Name), the
  buckets can tell "you working" from "anyone at a laptop". Not named? It still works, just a little
  looser.
- **Clips in iCloud** download when a reel needs them. If one takes too long, drag it from the album
  into your Reel Studio `inbox` folder instead.
- **Adding more later:** drop clips into an album or bucket folder any time. Every few months, say
  "update my b-roll" and the new footage gets sorted in.
- **Nothing leaves your computer.** Your library is read on your machine and no footage is uploaded.

The full list of what Claude runs is in `templates/broll/BROLL.md`, if you are curious.
