# Starter looks

Three ready-made looks: Editorial, Bold and Soft. Each sets the big-word font, the caption font, the colours and the pace. Pick one in the style picker for a single reel, or make one your default:

    node engine/reel.mjs look use editorial

Fonts are open-licensed Google fonts (licences in fonts/). Your own brand guide always wins: a look only fills in what your brand.json leaves out when you pick it for one reel.
