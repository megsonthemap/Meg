Drop raw footage and photos for your next reel here, then run `node engine/reel.mjs new <slug>`.
Files are moved into projects/<slug>/raw (video, audio) and projects/<slug>/assets (images).

You can also drag footage onto the review studio's "New reel" screen instead: it copies the files
in here for you and runs new + prep on its own. It stops there; the cut is still a conversation.
