# Reels by other creators Megs loves

- zoe_wanderingwaves: "Western Australia" in heavy white caps over b-roll, a 3D location pin sticker.
- thecreative.social: three-layer title (small handwritten kicker, big chunky pastel caps, stars
  doodled around it); "butter yellow" in the handwritten font on its own.
- studiomelrose: "day in the life" in a fat soft-pink serif, italic serif line under it with emoji.
- hannahelizzzy (type B advice): bold cream caps title, handwritten line under it; one-word
  captions low in the frame ("with").
- hannahelizzzy ($100K): handwritten kicker "CHAPTER #49 of…", huge butter-yellow serif caps,
  handwritten line under it; one-word captions ("six"). A running series number.

## The thread through all of them

Three layers in the top half: a small handwritten kicker, one big loud title, a small
handwritten or italic line underneath. Butter yellow or soft cream for the loud part, white for
the hand-written parts. Little doodles (stars) around the title. One-word captions, low and small,
so the title does the shouting. Series framing ("Chapter #49", "Day 2 of").
