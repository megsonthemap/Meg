## Her style, read from the screenshots

- **Title block, top third, centred.** A big title with a small qualifier line under it
  ("St Kilda Penguins" / "In Melbourne for free", "Travel to the UK" / "After 10 months in Australia").
- **Big title type:** chunky rounded heavy sans in butter yellow (CapCut "Kak"); sometimes an
  elegant cream serif with italic words mixed in (Puffing Billy), sometimes hand lettering.
- **Small line:** thin handwritten / doodle font in white (CapCut "Bulb"), often ending in emoji.
- **Captions:** one word at a time, chunky rounded sans with an outline, low-middle of the frame
  ("just", "oldest", "Pier"). Butter yellow or cream, sometimes deep red.
- **Footage:** handheld wide-angle selfie, walking and talking, tilted horizons, motion blur,
  lots of energy. Places shot as b-roll (the summer house kitchen) with the title over them.
- **Hook formula in the titles:** place + a reason to care ("for free", "after 10 months",
  "100 days left", "Day 2 of").
- Contact line on screen: contact@megsonthemap.com (small, above the title, penguins reel).
