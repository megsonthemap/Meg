# Effects that recreate Megs's look

Picked from library/catalog.json against her reels and the ones she loves. These go into her
brand guide as defaults to offer, not a fixed set: every reel still gets chosen from its own lines.

| Effect (as said out loud) | id | The kind of line it sits on |
|---|---|---|
| a small opening line above your head | hook-top | Her title block: big place name, small reason to watch under it |
| a quiet lead-in over one huge word | cap-phrase | "In Melbourne for… FREE" moments, the one word that sells the reel |
| one word at a time | cap-word | Her captions all the way through |
| a name tag under what you said | label | Place names as she arrives ("St Kilda Pier", "Day 2 · Asia fund") |
| a number counting up | counter | "100 days left", "10 months", money saved for the Asia fund |
| questions filling in one at a time | checklist | "Things to do in Tassie" lists |
| photos snapping in, one flash at a time | deck-snap | Snack hauls, everything she packed, every stop of the day |
| a screenshot or clip rising up behind you | backdrop | B-roll of the place she is talking about |
| a receipt of what it costs, then struck out | receipts | "For free", what a trip cost, budget travel |
| a doodle outline drawn around you | doodle | Light, funny moments (matches the doodled stars she loves) |
| the screen tears like paper | rip | Jumping to a new place or a new day |
| one giant word behind your head | bigword | The one word the reel hangs on ("HOME", "FREE"); Mac only |
