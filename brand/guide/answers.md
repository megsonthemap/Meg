# Megs's brand guide interview

## 1. Who and why
- Audience: women solo travellers and backpackers; people interested in content creation; and
  people back home following the trip.
- Goal: reach more people and get brands' attention. She is building UGC content creation in
  travel and tech, so every reel doubles as a portfolio piece for brands.

## 2. Sound and the cut
- Sound effects wanted, on the moment they match: mouse click, follow notification, whoosh/wave,
  typing. The engine plays sfx from edit.json (`sfx: [{src, at, volume}]`); she supplies the
  sound files (brand/sfx/), the kit ships none.
- Music: a trending track under the whole reel, always quiet under her voice; her voice always
  wins. Trending Instagram sounds are licensed inside the app, so the default is to render with
  her voice clean (or a quiet bed from her own music library) and add the trending sound in
  Instagram at low volume. Say this when a reel needs music.
- Cut tight: take out every gap and pause. Some shots only 0.1 to 0.2 seconds long, in quick
  bursts, to grab attention (especially the opening and location jumps).
- Nothing she has ruled out yet.
- Wants travel stickers: plane ticket, backpack, passport, suitcase, location pin, all travel
  things, popping in on the line that mentions them (`sticker` effect, PNGs in brand/shelf/stickers/).
  The location-pin sticker over a place name (from zoe_wanderingwaves) fits here too.

## 3. Formats
1. "Come with me" guides: a place or experience, often free or cheap (penguins, Puffing Billy).
2. Trip diaries / series: "2 weeks in Tasmania", "Day 2 of Asia fund".
3. Life updates and stories: "100 days left on my visa", "I'm coming home" (voice over).
4. Hauls and lists: "Aussie snacks for home", "things to do in Tassie".
5. UGC examples: brand-style travel and tech pieces made to show brands what she can do.

## 4. Brands and ideas
- Brands she wants: Airbnb, hotels, hostels, eSIM providers, backpacker brands, travel and tech brands.
