Who it's forEvery reel has three jobs

  • Useful to women travelling solo and backpacking. A real place, a real price, a reason to save it.
  • Warm for people back home. It's my life, not an advert.
  • A portfolio piece a brand would pay for. Clean enough that an Airbnb, hostel, eSIM, travel or tech brand could repost it as UGC.

Three seconds in, someone should feel: I want to go there with her.

The lookRead from my own reels

  • Handheld, wide-angle selfie, walking and talking. Tilted horizons and motion blur are part of it; never straighten or stabilise them away.
  • Out and about. Streets, piers, train platforms, car parks, hostel kitchens. Places get their own shots with a title over them.
  • Natural light, true colour. No heavy grade. Blue sky stays blue, skin stays warm.
  • Energy. Quick, playful, smiling. Never slow and moody.

TypeBig and loud on top, small and handwritten underneath

Loud: the headline

St Kilda penguins

In CapCut this is Kak: chunky, rounded, heavy. In the studio it renders in Avenir Next Heavy until I add a font file of my own. Always ALL CAPS.

Quiet: the line under it

in melbourne for free 🐧

In CapCut this is Bulb: thin and handwritten. In the studio it renders in the quiet font (Georgia) until I add a handwritten font file. Always lower case, emoji welcome at the end.

CapCut's Kak and Bulb live inside CapCut and can't be copied out. If I buy matching font files, they go in brand/fonts/ and every reel switches to them.

Also allowed now and then: an elegant serif with one or two words in italic (like the Puffing Billy title), or hand lettering for one-off titles.

CaptionsOne word at a time

  • One word on screen at a time, timed to my voice.
  • Display font, butter yellow with a thin chocolate outline; white on very yellow or bright footage.
  • Small and low so the headline does the shouting.
  • Captions step aside while a big graphic (list, receipt, counter) is up.
  • Not shouted: captions are never ALL CAPS sentences.

The cutTight, fast, no dead air

  • Every gap and pause out. Keep about 0.05 s of air either side of a word so it doesn't clip.
  • Flash cuts: bursts of 0.1 to 0.2 s clips at the start and whenever I jump to a new place or day.
  • Something changes on screen every 1.5 to 3 seconds.
  • The first frame already has the headline on it. The hook lands in the first second.
  • Expect the final cut to be 30 to 40% shorter than the raw take.
  • B-roll of the place goes full screen or rising up behind me, and only over the words that name it.

SoundMy voice always wins

  • Sound effects on the moment they match: mouse click, follow notification, whoosh or wave, typing. Files live in brand/sfx/.
  • Music under the whole reel, always quiet under my voice.
Trending Instagram sounds are licensed inside the app. Default: the reel is exported with my voice clean, and I add the trending sound in Instagram with its volume turned right down. If I want music baked in, Claude uses a track I own, sitting well under my voice.

FormatsThe five kinds of reel I make

1 · "Come with me" guides

A place or experience, often free or cheap (penguins, Puffing Billy). Headline = the place. Line under = the reason (for free, in Melbourne). 20 to 45 s.

2 · Trip diaries and series

"2 weeks in Tasmania", "Day 2 of Asia fund". Kicker carries the series number. Flash cuts between stops. 30 to 60 s.

3 · Life updates and stories

"100 days left on my visa", "I'm coming home". Often voice over. Slower than the rest, still no dead air. 20 to 60 s.

4 · Hauls and lists

"Aussie snacks for home", "things to do in Tassie". Photos snapping in, a list filling in, stickers. 20 to 40 s.

5 · UGC examples for brands

Travel and tech pieces made to show brands what I can do. Cleaner and more polished: product clearly shown, benefits said out loud, one tidy headline, no inside jokes. 15 to 30 s, repostable by the brand.

ShootingHabits that make the edit better

  • Film a 3 to 5 second clip of every place as I arrive: that's the b-roll and the flash cuts.
  • Start talking with "come with me to…" so the hook is already said.
  • For UGC pieces, film the product close up in good light, in use, from two angles.
I haven't said my filming setup yet. From the reels it looks like a phone on the wide lens, handheld. Claude will check when the first footage arrives.

In CapCutThe same look, made by hand on my iPad

Headline

  • Text → font Kak, ALL CAPS, colour #FFEFC3 (type the hex or use the eyedropper on an old reel).
  • Centred in the top third, 2 lines max, on screen from the first frame.

Line under it

  • Font Bulb, lower case, white, about a third of the headline's size, emoji at the end.
  • Optional small line above the headline in the same style ("day 2 of").

Captions

  • Text → Auto captions, then pick a word-by-word style: bold, butter yellow, thin chocolate #4E3C3C outline.
  • Drag them to about three quarters of the way down, above Instagram's caption area.
  • Fix any word it mishears; British spelling.

The cut

  • Remove every pause (use Remove silences / Auto cut if my CapCut has it, otherwise split and delete).
  • Opening and place changes: a burst of clips 0.1 to 0.2 s each.

Sound

  • Audio → Sound effects: search "mouse click", "notification", "whoosh", "typing". Put each on the exact moment.
  • Trending music from CapCut or Instagram: music volume about 10 to 15, my voice 100.

Stickers

  • Stickers: search "plane ticket", "backpack", "passport", "suitcase", "location pin". Beside me, never over my face.
Save the headline, the line under it and the caption style as favourites the first time, so every reel starts from them.

Hand me footageHow Claude edits for me

  1. Read this guide, brand/brand.json, brand/voice.json and brand/rules.json first.
  2. Describe every clip by what it shows. A clip nobody described is not used.
  3. Find my "come with me" line and build the opening on it: headline in caps in the top third, the lower-case line under it, on screen from frame one.
  4. Cut every gap. Flash-cut the opening and every place change.
  5. Offer me four to six effects, each quoting the line it sits on, in plain words. I choose. If I said "just cut it", choose a few and tell me which.
  6. Ask me how this reel ends. Don't assume.
  7. Sound effects only on the moment they match. My voice always on top.
  8. Every word on screen comes from what I said in the footage or from My words. British spelling.
  9. Never hand me a cut that fails the check.
